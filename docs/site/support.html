Low Hanging Fruit — Support

Need help with LHF? Email lowhangingfruit.help@gmail.com and include your app version (Settings shows it) and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Canvas login trouble?

  1. Give it a moment — Penn's sign-in can be slow, and the app recovers from most hiccups on its own if you don't re-tap.
  2. If it's stuck, tap Start over on the sign-in screen.
  3. Still stuck? Tap Report a problem on the error card — it opens an email with a diagnostics report attached. The report contains only technical routing info (page addresses and status codes), never your password, cookies, or personal data.
  4. You can always connect without logging in: choose Use calendar link instead and paste your Canvas calendar feed URL.

Something else?

Missing assignments, widget issues, feature ideas — email us. It's a small project and real humans read the inbox.
