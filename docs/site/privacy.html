Low Hanging Fruit — Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026

Low Hanging Fruit ("LHF") is a student assignment tracker that runs entirely on your device. The short version: we do not collect, store, sell, or share any of your data. We couldn't — the app has no server.

What the app does with your data

LHF connects your device directly to the services you choose (Canvas, and optionally Gradescope) to fetch your own assignments, deadlines, and grades. That data is stored only on your device. Nothing about you or your coursework is ever transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Credentials

When you sign in to Canvas or Gradescope inside the app, your login happens directly with those services in a secure web view — your password is never visible to the app and never leaves the sign-in page. Session cookies and your Canvas calendar feed address are stored in the iOS Keychain, Apple's encrypted credential store, on your device only.

Analytics and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no telemetry, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Data deletion

Everything the app knows lives on your device. Disconnecting a service in the app deletes its session and data; deleting the app removes everything else. There is nothing to delete on any server, because there is no server.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version ships, with the effective date revised.
