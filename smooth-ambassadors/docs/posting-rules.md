# posting rules

Keep ambassadors, the app, and the platforms safe. These are the tips
shown on every ambassador page (`messages.json` → `posting_tips`), with the
reasons.

## apple: never touch ratings or reviews

The App Store Review Guidelines, section 3 (Business), as published at
developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines on 2026-11-09:

> "If we find that you have attempted to manipulate reviews, inflate your
> chart rankings with paid, incentivized, filtered, or fake feedback, or
> engage with third-party services to do so on your behalf, we will take
> steps to preserve the integrity of the App Store, which may include
> expelling you from the Apple Developer Program."

And 5.6.3 Discovery Fraud:

> "Manipulating any element of the App Store customer experience such as
> charts, search, reviews, or referrals to your app erodes customer trust
> and is not permitted."

So: the role description no longer asks for a five-star review. No perk,
title, shout-out or milestone is ever tied to a review. The only allowed
line is: *if you like it, leave an honest review*, kept apart from
everything else. Inside the app, use Apple's own review prompt API
(5.6.1), never a custom one.

## anonymous campus apps (fizz, sidechat, yik yak)

Their guideline pages could not be fetched from this session (blocked
hosts: fizz.social, sidechat.lol, yikyak.com). Check them once before the
first posts. Until then the safe defaults, which match every such
community's spam rules:

- one post per app per campus moment. no reposting the same text.
- if it gets downvoted or removed, leave it. don't argue, don't repost.
- some apps strip links or hide posts with links. lead with "smooth for
  students" so people can search it, link second.
- if asked, say you're an ambassador. anonymity isn't a licence to pretend
  to be a random user.
- never post from multiple accounts or ask friends to upvote.

## posters on campus

- only on boards and walls the campus allows. student centres usually
  have a stamp or approval desk; dorms have RA rules.
- never over someone else's poster, never on glass, doors or trees.
- black and white, as designed. keep the qr code clear and unobstructed.
- log every drop on your page so we know where they are (and can take
  them down at semester end if needed).

## texting

Reminders come from a founder's own phone to a friend who said yes. No
bulk texting service unless Olisa decides to pay for one and set up
consent and opt-out properly (TCPA). "pause" or "stop" in a reply is
honoured immediately.
