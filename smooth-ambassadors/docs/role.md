# smooth campus ambassador

**what smooth is.** an iphone app that pulls your assignments and deadlines
from canvas, gradescope and ed discussion into one place, so you spend less
time keeping track of school and more time enjoying college. built by
students at penn.

app store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/smooth-for-students/id6783911002

**what you'd do.** 15 minutes to a couple of hours, your call, over a
semester:

1. download smooth, connect canvas, tell us what was confusing.
2. drop one of our ready-made messages in a couple of chats you're already
   in: class year, dorm, a club, your instagram story. your link is yours,
   so you'll see the opens.
3. post once in your school's anonymous app (fizz, sidechat, yik yak),
   where the rules allow it.
4. optional: put up a poster or two where your campus allows posters. we
   send the file, you print it, you log where it went (10 seconds).

**what you get.**
- founding ambassador for smooth at your school, on your résumé and
  linkedin, with a real recommendation from us after a semester.
- a direct line to the founders. your feedback actually changes the app.
- early builds before they ship, and a say in what we build next.
- your school's requests get priority.
- free merch for every ambassador once smooth passes 10,000 downloads.
  you can watch the count with us.

**what we never ask.** we never ask for ratings or reviews, and nothing
here depends on them. if you like the app, an honest review helps, and
that's entirely separate from this. apple's rules forbid incentivised
reviews and it would put the app at risk. (previous version of this doc
said "drop a 5 star review". that's gone.)

**how to start.** text marco with your name and school: +1 202 320 3802.
you'll get a private link to your own page: posters, messages, your
numbers. no account, no password.

**honestly.** this is a chill commitment with a good message about
enjoying college. if it ever feels like a chore, say so and we'll dial it
down. no pressure, ever.

marco and olisa, stem forward co.
